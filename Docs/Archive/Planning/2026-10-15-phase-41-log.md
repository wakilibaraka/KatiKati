# Phase 41 — Vertical layout modes, rail space use, tighter gaps

> Owner 2026-10-15: *"all layout mode changes should also apply vertically. design them to look well
> vertically. maximise space use. reduce gaps in all modes while keeping them accurate as per split mode."*
> Log written 2026-10-15 at slice end (not per step — see §Notes). Plan: `HYBRID_PLAN.md` §Phase 41.

## Scope in

- **41a** Every layout mode renders on a rail (delete `StripPlacement.effectiveMode`, drop
  `IslandSlotSet.modeForDisplay`).
- **41b** The rail's apps island fills the span between the fixed groups; a rail's chip stride is the rail's
  own 44 pt pitch; the drawer capsule keeps its slot.
- **41c** Tighter chrome in all modes: `IslandChrome.contentInset` 20 → 16,
  `StripContextMenuZone.dividerGapWidth` 13 → 9, `DockSpacing.comfortable` 10/12 → 8/10.
- **41d** Mode cards are portrait on a rail.

## Scope out

- No new setting, switch, string, dependency or file. `project.pbxproj` and `Localizable.xcstrings` untouched.
- No top edge. No theme/visual restyle. Chip identity, ordering, drag/drop, optimistic states untouched.
- `windows` / `centered` keep their definitions on a rail (no new vertical variants).
- The Block 1 vinyl-disc Now Playing and Phases 39a/39d stay where the plan has them.

## Decisions taken with the owner (before any code, Standing Rules §1.4)

1. Every mode renders on a rail — the Phase 18 fold goes.
2. The apps island absorbs the rail's slack (fixed groups hug the ends) rather than the islands spreading
   evenly with equal gaps.
3. Gaps/chrome tighten in **all** modes, the bottom bar included, with solver and view still reading one set
   of numbers — that shared source is the fixed meaning of "accurate as per split mode".

## Notes

- The solver's new `appsTrailingClearance` (default 0) was the one structural surprise: the fill exposed a
  pre-existing centring bug where an apps island that consumes its span could be pushed up to a whole capsule
  slot toward the trailing group. On a rail that would have put the drawer capsule on the clock/widgets
  island; the bottom bar had the same latent overlap on a nearly full row. Pinned by
  `RailGeometryTests.testRailAppsIslandFillsTheSpanAndKeepsTheCapsuleSlotClear`.
- `AppSettingsStore.defaultIslandGap/Margin` are now derived from `DockSpacing.comfortable`; while both read
  10/12 the fresh install matched the Comfortable card by coincidence, and dropping only the preset would
  have made a fresh install read **Custom**.
- §1.8's per-step cadence was not met for this phase (one session, notes at slice end) — same deviation as
  the Phase 12 close-out, recorded rather than hidden.
